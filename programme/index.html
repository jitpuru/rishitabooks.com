The Publishing Programme

One programme, wherever you are

Edited, designed, produced and distributed to the same standard — whether you want a thousand copies on your shelf or none at all. You keep full copyright to your work. Always.

What the programme includes

From idea to publication

Author coaching — from the idea through to an agreed outline and chapter structure, however many sessions that takes
Developmental and line editing
Interior layout and typesetting
Cover design — front cover and full print wrap
ISBN registration for paperback and ebook, under the Rishita Books imprint
Ebook production and listing on Amazon, Apple Books, Google Play and Kobo, including public library availability
Worldwide paperback availability, print-on-demand
Trade metadata and Nielsen title record
Project management through to publication day

Printed copies for you

Your book is available worldwide without a print run. If you want physical copies of your own — for talks, clients or launches — we can arrange them, delivered in Singapore or anywhere else. There is no minimum, and small quantities are entirely workable.

Tell us the country and the number, and we will quote.

Bookshops

Selected titles are stocked in bookshops across Singapore, Malaysia and Brunei through our distributor, at our cost. We make that call title by title, and we will tell you where your book stands.

What sits with you

Launch and marketing are yours. We give you everything you need — files, artwork, metadata, advance copies — and we are happy to advise, but selling the book is the author's work, and authors who plan for it early do considerably better.
