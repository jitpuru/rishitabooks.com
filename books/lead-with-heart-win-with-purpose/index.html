About the book

Success is never just about strategy — it is about who you are and how you show up for others. Across 18 transformative chapters, Kenny Lim unpacks, with humility and clarity, the key lessons behind building multiple businesses without ever losing sight of purpose, people and personal growth.

From overcoming perfectionism and rejection to mastering servant leadership and building teams that thrive without you, this is a guide to creating businesses that do not just generate revenue — they create real impact.

About the author

Kenny Lim

Kenny Lim

Founder · Servant Leader · Speaker

Kenny Lim is a respected industry leader and the founder of multiple thriving businesses across diverse sectors. A seven-figure income earner with over a decade of experience in sales leadership and business strategy, he leads by example — proving that sustainable success comes from serving others first.

Known for his directness, warmth and servant-leadership approach, Kenny is a sought-after speaker and mentor who continues to inspire the next generation of entrepreneurs.
