About the book

The core premise is simple but powerful: rejection is not a setback — it is a catalyst for growth. Drawing on over a decade in personal development and sales psychology, Jit Puru offers salespeople a way to maintain resilience and motivation in the face of every “no.”

Written for insurance advisors, entrepreneurs and sales leaders, this is a field guide to sustaining your drive without burning out — full of real-world stories and practical strategies you can use today.
