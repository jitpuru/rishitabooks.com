About the book

Great salespeople are not lucky — they are deliberate. Winning By Design lays out eight core principles for high-performance selling, from discovering your purpose and mastering your time to building the mental resilience that sustains a long career.

Grounded in visualisation, disciplined daily routines and systems that strengthen client relationships, this is a practical playbook for anyone ready to move from good to great.

About the author

Magesh Das brings over 20 years of financial services experience. He has qualified for the Million Dollar Round Table 18 times, earned Court of the Table honours four times, and reached Top of the Table status — placing him among the top 1% of advisers globally.

Beyond finance he is a single father, entrepreneur and National Champion bodybuilder, and the founder of a financial practice, a youth football academy and a life-coaching company.
