About

A boutique house with a bigger belief

Founded in Singapore to help experts and professionals publish books that carry real authority — and keep the rights to their own ideas.

Rishita Books was founded by author and publisher Jit Puru on a simple conviction: a well-made book is the most enduring form of authority a person can build — and it should belong to its author.

Too many capable experts either never finish their book, or hand it to a service that strips them of ownership and standards alike. We built Rishita Books to be the alternative: a boutique publishing house that brings a publisher's craft and a partner's care to every title, while the copyright stays entirely with the author.

We run one programme, wherever in the world you are. From author coaching and developmental editing through design, ISBN registration, production and worldwide distribution, we carry the book from first conversation to publication day — and we take our authors' ideas further through foreign rights.
